**Yuichi HIROI, Ph.D.** is a Senior Research Scientist at [Cluster Metaverse Lab](https://lab.cluster.mu/en/), [Cluster Inc.](https://corp.cluster.mu/en/), Japan, and a Visiting Researcher at the [Yuta Itoh Lab](https://www.ar.comp.isct.ac.jp/), Institute of Science Tokyo.

He received his B.Eng. and M.Eng. from [Keio University](https://www.ics.keio.ac.jp/) in 2015 and 2017, respectively, and joined [NTT Service Evolution Laboratory](https://www.rd.ntt/e/svlab/) in 2017. After leaving NTT, he entered the doctoral program at the School of Computing, [Tokyo Institute of Technology](https://educ.titech.ac.jp/cs/eng/) (now Institute of Science Tokyo), in 2019 and received his Ph.D. in Engineering in 2022. He then served as a JSPS Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Interfaculty Initiative in Information Studies, [the University of Tokyo](https://www.iii.u-tokyo.ac.jp/), before joining Cluster in 2023. He has also held his visiting appointment at Institute of Science Tokyo since 2026.

He aims to expand human imagination and creativity through his research, especially on visual experiences, which spans mixed reality, display optics, vision measurement and augmentation, metaverse community analysis, and AI support for creative and exploratory activities. His work has appeared in journals including IEEE TVCG and Optics Express and at conferences including IEEE VR and ISMAR, and has received awards at IEEE VR, Augmented Humans, and SIGGRAPH.
